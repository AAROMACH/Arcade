DISPATCH BOARD

You run dispatch for one workday, 7:00a to 6:00p. Jobs pop up around town. Send the right tech so they get there and finish before it's due. Make the most money you can.

Every job, 3 taps

  1. 1
    Tap a job — a pin on the map or a card under Open tickets.
    The colored circle is the skill it needs.
  2. 2
    Find a tech with that skill. Match the chip on the job to the chips by their name.
    Job CAM→● Luis AV CAM ✓
  3. 3
    Tap that tech. Their van drives over, does the job, and you get paid. Techs can line up several jobs; they do them in order. A tech booked solid past the due time can't take it.

What you get paid

Right skill, on time100%
Right skill, finished late50%
Wrong skill (takes 1.5× as long)40% − $75
Nobody sent before it's due−$150

Reading the map

Green outline: plenty of time
Yellow: under 2 hours left
Red, pulsing: under 1 hour. Send someone now
Faded, in a tech's color: already assigned

The tag over each pin counts down to the due time. Across town = longer drive, so pick the closest tech who has the skill.

Phone calls

A few times a day someone calls: a customer, your crew, a supplier. The game pauses. Pick an answer (tap it, or press 1–3). Answers can make or cost money and move two meters:

  • Customer trust and Crew morale both start at 60. At day's end, each point above 60 is +$10 to your score and each point below is −$10.
  • People remember what you told them on later days. Tap ⓘ by a tech's name to meet them.

End of the day

At 6:00p the day ends. Jobs not finished don't pay. Your day score = money billed + trust and morale bonuses.
