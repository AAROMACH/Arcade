Tone & Trace

A drop is dead. Work out why from the evidence, then pick the fix. Six tickets a shift; make $1,500 to meet the shift goal.

How it works

  • Each test costs minutes. Its result goes in your field notes.
  • Under 15 minutes pays the full $250, plus $100 if your first fix works. After 15 minutes, every minute costs $10.
  • A wrong fix costs 12 minutes and $40. Two wrong fixes, or 45 minutes, and the ticket is escalated: −$100.
  • Tickets get harder as the shift goes on, with more possible causes.

Think like a tech

  • The customer's story is a lead, not a fact. It's often wrong.
  • Many faults look the same on one test. A dark switch light could be the cable, the port, the patch cord, or the device itself. Find a second test that splits them.
  • The certifier gives the distance to a fault, measured from the jack. Compare it to the run's length.
  • If a laptop works at the jack, the cabling and the port are fine. Look at what's plugged in, or what's powering it.
  • A link light can be up and the drop still broken.
