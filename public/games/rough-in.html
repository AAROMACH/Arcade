Rough-In Day

The drywall goes up tomorrow. Get every low-voltage box in place today. You only get a quick look at the print, so remember it.

How it works

  • The blueprint shows where each box goes: data, camera, Wi-Fi AP, TV. Study it before the timer runs out.
  • Then the print goes back in the truck. Pick a box type and tap the spot on the floor where it goes. Tap a box again to take it off.
  • Normal: peek at the print any time for $75. Each peek shows it for 2 seconds.
  • Hard: shorter study time and only 3 peeks for the whole job, 1.5 seconds each. Scores x1.5.
  • Floors start small (3 data drops) and add boxes and box types as you go: cameras on floor 2, APs on 4, TVs on 6.
  • Any missed box is a callback and costs a hard hat. Lose all 3 and you're off the job.
  • The grid has letters across the top and numbers down the side, so you can remember spots like "data at C4".
  • Call inspection when you're done. Right box, right spot: +$150. Right spot, wrong box: +$50. A box that isn't on the print: −$75. A missed box means a callback after the drywall's up: −$150.
  • Near miss: the right box one square up, down, left or right of where it belonged (not diagonal) still counts as missed, but you get $75 back.
