TOOL DRAWER

Someone dumped the drawer on the bench. Put every tool back so the drawer is completely full: no gaps, nothing left over.

Controls

  • Drag a tool into the drawer. It snaps into the foam.
  • Tap a tool to turn it 90°. Flip mirrors the last tool you touched. Keys: R turns, F flips.
  • Drag a tool back to the bench to pull it out.
  • Hint flashes where one tool goes, but costs 10 seconds.

Two ways to play

  • Timed shift: drawers get bigger as you go, each on a clock. Run out of time and you can Continue from that drawer with the score you had going in, twice per shift. Scores go on the leaderboard.
  • Big drawer: one giant drawer, about 40 items, no clock. Hints are free. Your progress saves on this device, so you can come back to it. Your fastest time stays on this device, not the leaderboard.

The drawers

  • Every tool takes up the space its real shape does: the hammer's head sticks out, the drill is an L, the wrench has an open jaw.
  • There's always at least one way to fill the drawer exactly.
  • Odd shapes leave odd gaps, so drawers also hold small parts: hex keys, fuses, pencils, bits, wire nuts.
