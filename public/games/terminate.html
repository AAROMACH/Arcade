TERMINATE

Put the eight conductors in pin order, crimp it, and pass the tester. Do it fast: your 3 ft of slack shrinks while you work.

Rules

  • Tap a conductor to put it in the next empty pin. Tap a pin to pull its conductor back out.
  • The job card says which standard: T568B or T568A. Cables 1–4 are two of each with the color chart showing. From cable 5 the chart is hidden.
  • The shift clock runs while you work. Finish all 10 cables to post a time on the Fastest leaderboard.
  • Every cable has a 3 ft service loop, and it's your clock: it shrinks while you work. Peeking at the chart (2 seconds) costs 1 ft. A failed test lets you cut the bad end off and re-terminate for 1 ft, at half pay.
  • Out of slack, the cable's too short to save: that's a fail. 10 cables per shift; 3 fails and you're sent home.

Scoring

  • Pass: $100 plus up to $200 for the slack you have left. Passes in a row multiply it, up to ×3.
