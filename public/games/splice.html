SPLICE

Fuse all 12 fibers in the tray, in color order. Fibers 1–3 are easy, 4–6 medium, 7–9 hard, 10–12 super hard.

Each fiber

  • Cleave: the green window lands somewhere new on the dial every fiber, and it shrinks while you wait. Hit the bright center for a perfect cleave (+75).
  • Align X, then Y: the right core jitters, surges and stalls. Press when it lines up with the left one. On super hard, both cores move.
  • Arc: hold to fire. The fill rises at an uneven rate and the green band drifts. Let go when the fill's edge is inside the band.

Scoring

  • The splicer estimates the loss. ≤0.05 dB is great, ≤0.15 dB passes. Over that, you cut it out and do it again.
  • From hard on, the dial and the arc bar wander around while you aim.
  • 3 redos and the shift's over. Finish all 12 for a bonus, plus 250 for every redo you didn't use.
  • Keys: Space or Enter works too.
