RACK CLEANUP

You inherited a rat's nest. Clean up three racks a shift without taking the office down. Done means every cable is in its spot, the right color and length, and your mapping book is right.

Every port has a spot

  • Patch panel port N goes straight down to switch port N. Gear goes to its uplink: firewall → U1, NVR → U2, phone system → U3.
  • Cord color by what's on the far end: blue desk, red camera, yellow phone, green access point, white gear uplink. Use the shortest cord that reaches; extra length is slack and clutter.
  • Spare panel ports (nothing on the far end) get no cord. Pull any junk cords.

How

  • Tap a plug to unplug that end, then tap an empty port to plug it in. The link is down while you hold it. Tap where it came from to put it back.
  • Tap a cable to select it: swap the cord (color, length) or pull it out. Tap an empty port to tone it or patch a new cord.
  • Tone a panel port to find out what's on the far end. Labels can lie on later racks. Write it in your mapping book: drag the result into the row, or pick it from the row's list. Nothing checks it for you until the end.

Don't drop the network

  • Firewall, NVR and phone system are critical. Press Switch to backup first (it takes a few seconds), move their cable, then Return to primary.
  • Unplug critical gear without the backup and you have 15 seconds to plug it back in (−$25), or it's an outage: −$250 and −$5 a second until it's back.
  • Every desk, camera or phone that's unplugged costs $1 a second. Gear left on backup when you finish costs $50.

Pay

  • Pay scales with how tidy the rack is, plus $10 for each right mapping book row and −$15 for each wrong one. Minus downtime, outages, cords and time over par.
