FIND THE TOOL

Somewhere in this junk tote is the exact tool you need. Dig for it.

Moving things

  • Drag a tool to pick it up and move it.
  • Drag on empty space, or press and hold on a tool and then drag, to sweep with your hand. Tools slide, spin and shove each other out of the way.
  • Tap the tool you're looking for.
  • Tools have weight: a sweep nudges them, and big tools barely move.

Three ways to play

  • Timed shift: one tool at a time. Each find adds time and fills the tote higher. A wrong tap costs 3 seconds. Scores go on the leaderboard.
  • I Spy: a mountain of 140 tools and a checklist of 8. Find them in any order, no clock.
  • Drawer hunt (hard): a chest with 4 drawers, each with its own pile. There's only one of each tool you're sent for, in one drawer; the others hold look-alikes. Switching drawers costs 1 second, and the drawers stay how you left them, so remember what you saw. 90-second shift; each find adds time.
  • Watch for look-alikes: a blue screwdriver isn't the red one, and a Phillips isn't a flathead.
