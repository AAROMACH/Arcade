COVERAGE

The customer wants eyes on every red zone. Hang cameras so every zone tile is in view, using the fewest dollars you can.

Placing and removing cameras

  • Hang: pick Dome, Bullet or 360°, then tap a spot on the plan.
  • Aim: tap a camera to select it (you'll see exactly which tiles it covers). Tap it again to turn it 45°.
  • Move: drag a camera to a new spot.
  • Remove a camera, any of these ways:
    • Drag it off the edge of the plan and let go.
    • Press and hold it until a red ✕ appears, then tap the ✕.
    • On a computer: right-click it, or select it and press Delete or Backspace.
  • Clear takes every camera down at once.

Rules

  • Dome cameras ($180) see 100° but only 4 tiles out. Bullet cameras ($240) see 45° and 8 tiles out.
  • Walls and shelving block the view. Doorways don't.
  • Every camera takes one NVR channel. You can't go over the recorder's channel count.
  • From site 3: 360° fisheye ($300) sees all around, 3 tiles out. From site 4: the gate needs plate reads, so only a bullet camera counts there.
  • When every zone is green, sign off. The target cost is what a good installer would spend: come in at or under it for 3 stars.
