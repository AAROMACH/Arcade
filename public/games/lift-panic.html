HOW TO PLAY

Goal

You're on a scissor lift rolling through a warehouse. Hang as many cameras as you can before you take three hits.

Rules

  • Mount cameras: line the tech's hands up with each yellow MOUNT ring as it passes. Ceiling mounts are worth 100, column mounts 150.
  • Streaks: mounts in a row multiply the points, up to ×5. Missing one resets the streak but doesn't cost a life.
  • Dodge everything under Obstacles below. Only the tech counts as a hit (hard hat to boots), not the lift. Each hit costs a hard hat; three and the shift's over.
  • The lift is slow on purpose, so start moving early. The warehouse speeds up the longer you last.
  • Every camera can be reached — nothing ever blocks a mount point.

Obstacles

Hover or tap one to see how it moves. Orange outlines are exactly what hits you (only your tech counts, not the lift). The green arrow is the way past. Tagged ones show up later in a run.

Controls

  • ↑ / W raise · ↓ / S lower · P pause
  • Phone: hold RAISE or LOWER under the game (move them in ⚙ Settings), or hold the top / bottom half of the game.
