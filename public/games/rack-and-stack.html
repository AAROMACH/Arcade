How to play

Rack the gear as it drops. Fill a row to clear it. Don't let the rack fill to the top.

1
Every full row needs a switch.

A row clears only if it has a SWITCH in it, or touching it from above or below.

No switch? The row turns red: NO UPLINK. Put a switch against it to clear it. You get a bonus.

2
Heavy gear goes low.

Servers, UPSs and battery cabinets have an amber outline. Keep them under the dashed amber line.

Too high = 1 load warning. 3 warnings = game over.

3
Blue outline = you can turn it.

Amber (heavy) gear can't turn.

4
Bonus: patch it.

Put a patch panel right above or below a switch. That row is worth more when it clears.

Every 8 rows cleared = next level. Gear drops faster.

Difficulty

Easy: slow drop, for practice. Easy scores stay on this device and don't go on the leaderboard. Normal: standard speed. Hard: starts fast, speeds up quicker, and there's no pause. Each difficulty keeps its own best.
